# Reviewer instruction addendum — version 1.0

Applies identically to Reviewer 1 and Reviewer 2. Read together with `RUBRIC.md` in your
package. This addendum does not change the categories (C1–C5, U) or the sheet format.

Judge photographic or specimen identity using visual structures, not matching numbers, credits, logos or watermarks. Do not search for source images or consult external information. Work independently, use U when visual evidence is insufficient, and note visible markings that interfere with your judgement.
